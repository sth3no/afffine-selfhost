# Focal Threshold

A design philosophy for the AFFiNE Capture extension icon (v0.3).

---

## Movement

**Focal Threshold** — communication through the architecture of attention.
Two ideas held in one geometry: the brackets that *frame* and the warm
mark they *hold*. Capture is the gesture; the cookie is the residue. Both
live in the same glyph, and the glyph says nothing it does not need to.

## Visual expression

**Form.** A single rounded square — AFFiNE's chassis — and within it,
four corner brackets and one centered point. The brackets are not decoration;
they are an *act*. They close around what is now under the user's care. The
gesture is reductive in the Bauhaus sense: every line earns its place, and
every removal is a discipline.

**Color.** AFFiNE blue (#2B85FF) as the calm field. White brackets for
clarity at any size. A warm amber dot (#FFCB47) — descended from the v0.1
"yellow crumbs" but now centered, singular, deliberate. The amber is not
playful; it is the residue of attention, the data that persists. Two
hues, plus white. Three notes, struck cleanly.

**Composition.** The brackets sit at a measured inset, claiming the
periphery without crowding it. The dot rests at the optical center — not
the geometric center; even a one-pixel offset would betray master-level
restraint. Negative space carries the same weight as the marks themselves;
the frame breathes around the focal point.

**Scale.** The icon must read identically across an order of magnitude:
128 pixels in the Web Store, 16 pixels in a tab strip. At small sizes
the gradient yields to a flat field, the brackets thicken proportionally,
the dot grows by a fraction — micro-corrections invisible to the casual
eye but felt by the trained one. This is the work of countless careful
passes. Nothing is left to chance.

**Rhythm.** Four brackets. One dot. The eye traces the perimeter and
returns to the center. Symmetry on both axes — broken only by the soft
top-edge highlight that gives the surface its lift. The composition is
quiet enough to hold both meanings simultaneously: capture, and continuity.

## Craftsmanship

This icon is meant to look as if it was sweated over for a week and then
nothing was added. Every metric — corner radius, bracket inset, stroke
weight, dot diameter — is derived from a coherent ratio. The
supersampled-and-downsampled pipeline is not a shortcut but a discipline:
master at high resolution, refine until the small sizes earn their crispness,
ship only when 16 px is as legible as 128. The result should feel inevitable.
Someone seeing it for the first time should not notice it at all; only on
second look should they realize how little was needed to say so much.

## What this is not

Not a wordmark. Not a literal cookie illustration. Not a camera icon
borrowed from a stock library. It is a multitool — capture and sync —
expressed as one continuous gesture, fit for a self-hosted knowledge tool
that values precision and quiet.
